School of Emerging Technologies & Systems (SETS)

A national catalyst for scientific ascendancy and strategic independence

SETS serves as the flagship institution for advanced systems research and multidisciplinary technology development, functioning as a national catalyst for scientific ascendancy and strategic independence. Through its integrated ecosystem of specialized centres, it ensures interoperability and coherence across domains such as communications, autonomy, cybersecurity, quantum technologies, and space systems.

As a NASTP institution, SETS matures technologies to pre-integration readiness — enabling their transition into operational development programs and productization within the broader national innovation ecosystem.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be the nation's premier institution for high-impact applied research and innovation — advancing national security while fostering research-based innovative solutions across public and private sectors, and contributing to enduring scientific progress and technological ascendancy.

Mission

To generate new knowledge and lead strategic, high-impact research by fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, advancing indigenous technology development, and transitioning scientific breakthroughs into operational capabilities — strengthening national security, self-reliance, and technological leadership.

Our Role

SETS functions as the flagship institution for advanced systems research and multidisciplinary technology development, acting as a national catalyst for scientific ascendancy and strategic independence. Through its integrated ecosystem of specialized centres, it ensures interoperability and coherence across communications, autonomy, cybersecurity, quantum technologies and space systems — generating new knowledge and transitioning scientific breakthroughs into operational capabilities that strengthen national security, self-reliance and technological leadership.

Core Tasks

Eight mission-driven mandates define how SETS delivers national impact across the technology-readiness spectrum.

01

Execute high-impact, mission-driven research across core technology domains at TRL 1–4.

02

Develop integrated, multi-domain technological capabilities.

03

Drive indigenous technology development and self-reliance.

04

Accelerate innovation for technology incubation.

05

08

Advanced Research & Innovation Centres (ARICS)

SETS' Advanced Research & Innovation Centres conduct advanced scientific research, applied engineering, modelling and simulation, and prototype development and validation — maturing technologies to pre-integration readiness for transition into operational programs within the broader NASTP ecosystem. Centre initiation is prioritized in line with emerging needs and futuristic technology trends.

CAUAVRD

Centre for Anti-UAV Research & Development

  • Counter-UAS detection and tracking systems
  • Kinetic and non-kinetic defeat mechanisms
  • Autonomous perception and mission systems
  • AI-driven threat classification and response
  • Integrated air defence and C2 systems
CSHR

Centre for Super & Hypersonic Research

  • Hypersonic glide vehicle (HGV) design and aerodynamics
  • Rocket, scramjet and dual-mode ramjet propulsion
  • High-temperature materials and thermal protection systems
  • Hypersonic guidance, navigation and control
  • Computational fluid dynamics for high-Mach regimes
CABSP

Centre for Advanced Baseband Signal Processing

  • Waveform design and pulse/signal compression for comm & radar
  • Detection, estimation & tracking algorithms
  • Adaptive and digital beamforming and spectral solutions
  • Channel/clutter estimation, equalization and interference suppression
  • Real-time processing on FPGA/DSP/GPU; EW and comm jamming
CARFT

Centre for Advanced RF Technologies

  • AESA RF front-end design & development
  • T/R module design and RF power amplifier technologies
  • Antenna array design and RF/microwave integration
  • RF filter, mixer and frequency synthesis technologies
  • High-power microwave, directed energy and radar jamming
CGNC

Centre for Guidance, Navigation & Control

  • Flight control and precision guidance algorithms
  • Inertial navigation systems (INS) and GNSS integration
  • Terminal homing and seeker head design & signal processing
  • Anti-jamming and GNSS-denied navigation solutions
  • Autonomous waypoint navigation and trajectory optimization
CASEIS

Centre for Advanced Sensors, EO & IR Systems

  • Electro-optical and infrared sensor design
  • Digital beamforming and MIMO radar
  • Multispectral and hyperspectral imaging
  • Sensor fusion and data processing algorithms
  • Target acquisition, tracking and designation systems
CST

Centre for Space Technologies

  • Small satellite and CubeSat design and development
  • Space-based intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR)
  • Satellite communication systems and ground segment
  • Space situational awareness and debris tracking
CDST

Centre for Drone Swarm Technology

  • Swarm intelligence and distributed autonomy algorithms
  • Multi-agent coordination and mission planning
  • Swarm communication protocols and mesh networking
  • Collaborative target engagement and payload delivery
CAISDT

Centre for AI, Simulations & Digital Twin

  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning for defence applications
  • High-fidelity mission and battlefield simulation
  • Digital twin modelling of platforms and systems
  • Synthetic data generation and virtual test environments
CM

Centre for Materials

  • Lightweight structural composites and meta-materials
  • Radar-absorbing and low-observable materials
  • High-temperature and refractory materials for propulsion
  • Energetic and reactive materials research
  • Additive manufacturing and rapid prototyping of advanced materials
